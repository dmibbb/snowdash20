Objective

Oh no! Santa lost all his presents! Help him, Mr. Snejok!

TO WIN
Collect every present in the level.
TO HELP YOU
Eat snow to clear your path.
Push boulders off ledges to crush monsters.
WATCH OUT
Any monster kills you on touch.
So does a falling boulder.
LIVES
You start with 4.
Each death resets the level (presents return).
Lose them all, and it's Game Over.
